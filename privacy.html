Privacy Policy

Duskline has no accounts and does not operate an advertising profile. Preferences, favorites, cached weather, and an approximate location may be stored in your browser.

When you search or request weather, your browser contacts Open-Meteo and, for U.S. locations, the National Weather Service. If you allow browser location, Duskline rounds coordinates before saving or sending them. Those providers have their own policies.

Hosting infrastructure may process standard technical request data. You can clear local browser storage or deny location permission at any time.
